# History

Everything you dictated ends up here: text, recording and time.

## What this page does

- **Find text** and copy it with one click.
- **Listen to the recording** while it still exists. How long recordings are kept is set under Settings, General, Retention.
- **Transcribe again**, for example with a better model.
- **Keep**: marked entries survive the retention period.
- The button at the top right opens the recordings folder in the file manager.

## What lives elsewhere

Meetings and imported files are under Recordings. Read-aloud texts are pages on the Read aloud page.

## If something is missing

- **No entry after dictating**: the dictation was cancelled or empty. Check the microphone in the footer.
- **Recording cannot be played**: the retention period expired or the file was deleted by hand. The text stays.
